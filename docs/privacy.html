NearMe Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

NearMe uses location, preferences, and activity inside the app to recommend nearby events. We do not sell personal information, serve targeted ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who can use NearMe

NearMe is intended for adults age 18 and older. It is not directed to children.

Information we collect

  • Location: precise device location when you grant permission, or a city/address you enter. If you decline location access, you can choose a location manually.
  • Preferences: interests, purposes, availability, travel radius, budget, social energy, accessibility preferences, and filters you choose.
  • Activity inside NearMe: event impressions, opens, saves, hides and hide reasons, ticket/source clicks, directions, shares, and post-event feedback. We also record app version, session identifier, and timestamps needed to measure whether recommendations are useful.
  • Anonymous account identifier: Supabase creates a random authenticated identifier so your profile and activity are protected by per-user access rules. We do not require your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or social accounts.
  • Subscription status: RevenueCat tells us whether NearMe Plus is active. Apple processes payment; NearMe does not receive card details.

How we use information

  • To retrieve events inside the radius you selected and rank them for your constraints.
  • To learn from saves, dismissals, source clicks, directions, and outcome feedback.
  • To remember preferences and locally saved event snapshots.
  • To measure feed quality, retention, errors, and subscription performance.
  • To prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and improve event accuracy.

Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, event data, preferences, interactions, and product-quality analytics.
  • RevenueCat and Apple App Store: optional subscription status and payment processing.
  • Expo and native map services: app infrastructure, notifications, geocoding, and map display.
  • Anthropic: server-side extraction and discovery may receive a coarse search location, selected preferences, and public event or venue content. We do not send names, contact details, or Apple payment data.
  • Event sources: opening a ticket or official source link takes you to that third party, whose privacy policy then applies.

Sharing and advertising

We do not sell personal information or share it with data brokers or advertising networks. Sponsored event placement, if introduced, will be labeled and will not involve cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences and saved snapshots remain on your device until you clear them or delete the app. Server-side profile, interaction, and product-event records remain while you use NearMe or until deletion is requested. Operational logs and aggregated, de-identified statistics may be retained for security and product analysis.

Your choices

  • Change or revoke location and notification permissions in device Settings.
  • Change personalization constraints or clear local data from the You tab.
  • Manage NearMe Plus through Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Request access to or deletion of server-side data by emailing dbh28tekkit@gmail.com with the subject “NearMe data request.”

Security

NearMe uses encrypted network connections and Supabase row-level security. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so we limit collection to what is needed to run and improve the product.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as NearMe changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions can be sent to dbh28tekkit@gmail.com.
