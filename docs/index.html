FAQ

Does NearMe cost money?

Yes. NearMe is a subscription app and needs an active subscription to use. A weekly plan and an annual plan are offered, and the current price plus any free trial is shown in the app before you confirm. See the Terms of Service for renewal and cancellation details.

How does NearMe find events?

We combine official ticketing feeds, public venue and campus calendars, community listings, and publicly posted information. Some listings are produced by automated extraction, so we link every event back to its source when one exists and ask you to confirm the details that matter before you go.

Does NearMe work in my city?

Coverage varies by location. NearMe is strongest in South Florida today and is expanding city by city. If verified inventory is limited, the app tells you instead of silently showing another city.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, then NearMe, then Cancel. Your subscription stays active until the end of the billing period. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

Is my data safe?

Yes. NearMe only stores what's needed to serve you events. We don't sell your data, track you across apps, or share personal info with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I don't see any events near me — help?

Pull down to refresh, or raise your radius under You. At the default 10-mile setting, when your area is thin NearMe looks further out and labels anything beyond your radius with its real distance. If you choose a radius yourself, that choice is a hard limit and nothing outside it is shown. If the area still looks empty, email us so we can prioritize its sources.

Can I report an incorrect event?

Absolutely. Email us with the event name and what's wrong, and we'll fix it.
